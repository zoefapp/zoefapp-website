Trains represent an extraordinary mode of transportation: **fast, comfortable, quiet, and often electrically powered**. The extensive railway network offers unparalleled possibilities.

Nevertheless, there has been sharp criticism of train services for quite some time. Often, they are dirty, vandalized, expensive, and suffer from overcrowding. Moreover, their punctuality often leaves much to be desired. **Surely, this can be different, right?**

_"We aspire to a world where every train journey, regardless of the operator or country, excels in impeccable punctuality, sufficient seating, and an unparalleled level of cleanliness and comfort. Our goal is to transform the travel experience and set a new standard for train travel, making traveling by train the norm."_ - Zoef.app
