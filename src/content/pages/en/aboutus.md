Trains represent an extraordinary means of transportation: fast, comfortable, quiet, and often electrically powered. The extensive railway network offers unprecedented possibilities.

Nevertheless, there has been sharp criticism of train services for some time. They are often polluted, vandalized, expensive, and suffer from overcrowding. Moreover, they frequently fall short in terms of punctuality. Many passengers are also unaware of their rights. Consequently, they sometimes unjustly avoid trains, missing out on hundreds of euros per year.

It is our mission to support passengers when train journeys do not go as planned.
