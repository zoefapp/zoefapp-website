# The Common Form

On March 27, 2024, the European Commission adopted the regulation concerning the common form. This means that as of July 2, 2024, all train passengers can use a uniform form. Several specific rules apply:

- All carriers must accept this form
- Compensation must be paid within 30 days of the request
- Carriers must at least meet the rules set by the regulation but can also offer better conditions

# Automatic Processing

Processing the common form is manual work for many carriers. To simplify this work, Zoef.app offers the possibility to automate this process.

## Level 1: Convert to machine-readable format

The simplest service is to convert the submitted forms into a machine-readable format. This allows carriers to connect to their existing processing platform.

## Level 2: Outsource the process

Zoef.app offers a fully automated system where compensation requests are checked for validity. In some cases, this cannot be determined automatically, and in those cases, a case is created for a customer service representative to manually check the claim. Communication with the traveler and financial handling is then fully automated.
